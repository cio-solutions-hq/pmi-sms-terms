SMS messaging program

Peters and Milam Insurance Services

Peters and Milam Insurance Services sends text messages to people who choose to receive them. Messages may relate to insurance quotes, policies, claims, billing reminders, appointments, and customer support.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

Consent to receive SMS messages is not a condition of purchasing insurance products or services.
