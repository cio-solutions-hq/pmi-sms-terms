SMS Privacy Policy

Last updated October 7, 2026

This policy describes how Peters and Milam Insurance Services handles personal information collected for our SMS messaging program. It covers text-message opt-in, delivery, help, and opt-out. It is the privacy statement for that program.

Information we collect

When you opt in to SMS messages, we collect:

  • Your mobile phone number
  • Your consent to receive text messages, including the date, time, and method of opt-in when that information is available
  • Records related to the program, such as STOP, START, and HELP requests and messages needed to provide the service you requested

How we use this information

We use this information to send the messages you agreed to receive, including messages about insurance quotes, policies, claims, billing reminders, appointments, and customer support. We also use it to process opt-out and help requests and to keep a record of your consent.

No sharing of opt-in data for marketing

Mobile opt-in information and consent will not be shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

We do not sell mobile phone numbers or SMS consent records. We do not share your opt-in information or consent with third parties so that those parties can market their own products or services to you.

Service providers

We may provide your mobile number to vendors that transmit messages for us, including our messaging platform and wireless carriers, only so they can deliver the messages you agreed to receive and maintain the program. Those providers may use the information to deliver the service. They are not authorized to use your mobile opt-in information or consent for their own marketing.

Your choices

Reply STOP to opt out of SMS messages. Reply START to opt back in. Reply HELP for assistance. You may also call (805) 687-3225 or email info@petersmilam.com. Opt-out steps are described on the opt-out page.

Consent to receive SMS messages is not a condition of purchasing insurance products or services. See the SMS Terms and Conditions.
