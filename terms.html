SMS Terms and Conditions

Last updated October 7, 2026

These SMS Terms and Conditions apply to text messages sent by Peters and Milam Insurance Services (“we,” “us,” or “our”) to mobile numbers that have opted in to our SMS program.

Program description

By submitting your phone number, requesting a quote, completing a customer service form, or otherwise providing consent to Peters and Milam Insurance Services, you agree to receive text messages related to insurance quotes, policies, claims, billing reminders, appointments, and customer support.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out and HELP for assistance.

Consent

Consent to receive SMS messages is not a condition of purchasing insurance products or services.

You may refuse or withdraw consent at any time. Opting out of text messages does not cancel an insurance policy or affect your ability to request a quote or receive customer service by phone, email, or through our website.

By opting in, you confirm that you are the subscriber or customary user of the mobile number you provide, or that you have the subscriber’s permission to enroll that number.

Message frequency and charges

Message frequency varies based on your requests and the services you use. Message and data rates may apply. Your wireless carrier’s message and data rates apply to texts you send and receive. We do not charge a separate fee to join this SMS program.

Opt-out

Reply STOP to any program message to opt out. You will receive a confirmation, and you will no longer receive SMS messages from this number unless you opt in again. Reply START to opt back in at any time.

Additional details are on the opt-out page.

Help

Reply HELP for assistance, call (805) 687-3225, or email info@petersmilam.com. Program information is also available on the program home page.

Privacy

Mobile opt-in information and consent will not be shared with third parties for marketing purposes. Please read our SMS Privacy Policy.

Delivery

Message delivery is subject to your wireless carrier and device. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. This program is intended for mobile numbers that can receive SMS messages in the United States.

Changes

We may update these terms by posting a revised version on this page. The last-updated date shows when these terms were last changed. Continued participation after an update means you accept the revised terms. You may opt out at any time if you do not agree.
