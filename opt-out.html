Opt out of SMS messages

STOP, START, and HELP

You can stop text messages from Peters and Milam Insurance Services at any time. Opting out applies to this SMS program. It does not cancel an insurance policy.

STOP

Reply STOP to a message from this program to opt out. You can also text STOP to the number that sent you the message.

After you opt out, you should receive this confirmation:

You have successfully opted out of SMS messages from Peters and Milam Insurance Services. You will no longer receive messages from this number. Reply START to opt back in at any time. Additional details are available at https://cio-solutions-hq.github.io/pmi-sms-terms/opt-out.html.

START

Reply START to opt back in at any time. After you opt back in, you may again receive messages about insurance quotes, policies, claims, billing reminders, appointments, and customer service. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

The opt-in confirmation is: “Thank you for opting in to receive SMS messages from Peters and Milam Insurance Services. You may receive messages regarding insurance quotes, policies, claims, billing reminders, appointments, and customer service. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for assistance or STOP to opt out.”

HELP

Reply HELP for assistance. You can also call (805) 687-3225 or visit the program home page.

The help response is: “Thank you for contacting Peters and Milam Insurance Services. For assistance, please call (805) 687-3225 or visit https://cio-solutions-hq.github.io/pmi-sms-terms/. Reply STOP to opt out of SMS communications.”
